The aim of the BioASQ Lab is to push the research frontier towards systems that use the diverse and voluminous information available online to respond directly to the information needs of biomedical scientists.
<!--more-->

## Tasks

The BioASQ Lab features six different tasks:

{{< accordion  open_all="true" >}}
  {{< accordion-item title="Biomedical Semantic Question Answering" >}}
  Benchmark datasets of biomedical questions, in English, along with gold standard (reference) answers constructed by a team of biomedical experts. The participants have to respond with relevant articles and snippets from designated resources, as well as exact and "ideal" answers.
  {{< /accordion-item >}}
  {{< accordion-item title="Synergy: Question Answering for developing problems" >}}
  Biomedical experts pose unanswered questions for developing problems, such as COVID-19, receive the responses provided by the participating systems, and provide feedback, together with updated questions in an iterative procedure that aims to facilitate the incremental understanding of developing problems in biomedicine and public health.
  {{< /accordion-item >}}
  {{< accordion-item title="MultiClinSum-3: Entity-aware Multilingual Clinical Summarization" >}}
  A shared task on the automatic entity-aware summarization of lengthy clinical case reports written in different languages. The organizers distribute lengthy clinical case reports written in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, and German. The participants generate entity-aware summaries of the clinical case reports. The evaluation is based on a comparison with manual summaries of the clinical case reports.
  {{< /accordion-item >}}
  {{< accordion-item title="ElCardioCC: Clinical Coding in Cardiology" >}}
  The ELCardioCC 2027 shared task concerns the automatic assignment of cardiology-related ICD-10 codes to hospital discharge letters at the document level. The dataset was curated from discharge letters, which capture critical aspects of patient care, including diagnoses, symptoms, medical procedures, and treatment plans.
  {{< /accordion-item >}}
  {{< accordion-item title="GutBrainIE: Gut-Brain Interplay Information Extraction" >}}
  The GutBrainIE task aims to foster the development of Information Extraction (IE) systems that support experts by automatically extracting and linking knowledge from scientific literature, facilitating the understanding of gut-brain interplay and its role in neurological disease. The task is divided into two core subtasks: i) Named Entity Recognition and Disambiguation (NERD), and ii) Document-level Relation Extraction (RE).
  {{< /accordion-item >}}
  {{< accordion-item title="PQASD: Pharmaceutical Question Answering over Structured Data" >}}
  A shared task on the ability of AI agents to interpret complex scientific questions and translate them into structured queries. The dataset is based on the public instance of the Open Targets database and its GraphQL API.
  {{< /accordion-item >}}
{{< /accordion >}}
