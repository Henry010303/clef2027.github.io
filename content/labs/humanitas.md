**HUMANITAS** (*Historical Understanding and Multimodal Analysis of Noisy, Interoperable Textual and Audiovisual Sources*) is a lab at CLEF devoted to evaluating and benchmarking computational methods that support research, access, analysis, enrichment, and interpretation across Digital Humanities (DH) and Cultural Heritage (CH). Intended as a sustainable umbrella initiative within CLEF, it provides a space where evaluation methodologies and reusable, multilingual benchmark collections accumulate over time, acting as a benchmarking layer that complements European research infrastructures such as CLARIN and DARIAH.

<!--more-->

## Tasks

In its first edition at CLEF 2027, HUMANITAS adopts a **workshop format** rather than a predefined shared task (no system runs are submitted this year). Its main goal is community building, discussing evaluation needs, assessing available datasets, and producing a roadmap of priority task scenarios and evaluation frameworks for future campaign-style CLEF tracks.
