The eHealth 2027 Lab brings together researchers working on Natural Language Processing (NLP) for clinical texts, with two tasks focusing on clinical text de-identification and automatic Case Report Form (CRF) filling. The Lab provides datasets in four languages (French, Italian, German, and English), covering different clinical settings and types of documents, including real, synthetic, and fictitious-patient records. Through these shared tasks, we aim to encourage the development of NLP methods that can help protect patient privacy and make clinical information easier to process and reuse for research.

<!--more-->

## Tasks

The eHealth Lab features two different tasks:

{{< accordion open_all="true" >}}
  {{< accordion-item title="Task 1: Clinical Text De-identification" >}}
  This task focuses on identifying and removing personal and sensitive information from clinical documents to protect patient privacy while preserving the information needed for research and other secondary uses.
  {{< /accordion-item >}}

  {{< accordion-item title="Task 2: Automatic Case Report Form (CRF) Filling" >}}
  This task focuses on automatically extracting relevant information from clinical documents and using it to fill in structured Case Report Forms (CRFs), with the aim of reducing the time and effort required from healthcare professionals.
  {{< /accordion-item >}}
{{< /accordion >}}
